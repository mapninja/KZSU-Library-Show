The profile doesn't learn from its own picks.

How they count:

- Show and recap playlists weigh most (x3): Next Show, DJ Stace - 09/10/2026, Top 25 of 2026.

## How recon uses this profile

1. New releases get points for artists already in the profile (airplay, streams, reviews and playlists), related RIYL names, and preferred labels.
2. Artists aired in the past 21 days and tracks aired in the past 180 days are penalized.
3. Each suggestion lists its evidence (for example, "Drag City, aired 90 shows, 71 YTM plays in 90 days").
4. Scores are starting weights. Change them in `scripts/build_taste_profile.py`.
