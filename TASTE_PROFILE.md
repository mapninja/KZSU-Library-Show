## What your playlists add

Excluded: "God is not Great" (audiobook), New Episodes and Episodes for Later, plus Air Order, Working and Stace's Daily Playlist, which I build, so the profile doesn't learn from its own picks.

How they count:

- Show and recap playlists weigh most (x3): Next Show, DJ Stace - 09/10/2026, Top 25 of 2026.
- Liked Music, To Review and June-August Recap count x2. Love Songs and the FCC playlists count x1.5.
- All-Time Favorites, Night mix, Covers, Desert Sessions and Songs 2:00 and less count x1. Crawfish Boil counts x0.5 because it mostly duplicates All-Time Favorites.
- Soundbed, Karaoke and Halloweenie count x0.3. They're utility lists, not taste.
- Track counts are log-scaled, so 80 Guided By Voices tracks count about 6x one track, not 80x.

## How recon uses this profile

1. New releases get points for artists already in the profile (airplay, streams, reviews and playlists), related RIYL names, and preferred labels.
2. Artists aired in the past 21 days and tracks aired in the past 180 days are penalized.
3. Each suggestion lists its evidence (for example, "Drag City, aired 90 shows, 71 YTM plays in 90 days").
4. Scores are starting weights. Change them in `scripts/build_taste_profile.py`.
