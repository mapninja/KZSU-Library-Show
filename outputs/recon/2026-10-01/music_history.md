# This week in music history: Sept. 28 to Oct. 4, 2026

Research Sept. 26, 2026, from thisdayinmusic.com day pages, Wikipedia year lists, and cross-checks. Confidence: **H** = two or more sources agree, **M** = one source or minor conflict, **L** = unverified.

## Best eight for talk breaks

1. **Oct. 1, 1966 (60 years):** Hendrix sits in with Cream at London's Regent Street Polytechnic, about a week after arriving, and plays "Killing Floor." (H)
2. **Oct. 1, 1996 (30 years):** Nirvana's *From the Muddy Banks of the Wishkah* (DGC) and Silver Jews' *The Natural Bridge* (Drag City) come out the same day. *Wishkah* debuts at No. 1. (H)
3. **Sept. 28, 1976 (50 years):** Stevie Wonder, *Songs in the Key of Life*. A 50th-anniversary tour was announced for Oct. 13 to Dec. 19. (H)
4. **Sept. 29, 1986 (40 years):** The Fall's *Bend Sinister* and New Order's *Brotherhood* come out. Iggy Pop's *Blah-Blah-Blah* came out the same month; the exact day is M. (H/M)
5. **Oct. 1, 2007:** Radiohead announce the pay-what-you-want *In Rainbows*. (H)
6. **Bay Area:**
   - Oct. 2, 1967: police raid the Grateful Dead house at 710 Ashbury.
   - Bob Weir's *Blue Mountain* turns 10 on Sept. 30. Weir died Jan. 10, 2026.
   - Hardly Strictly Bluegrass runs Oct. 2-4. (H)
7. **Oct. 3, 1949:** Lindsey Buckingham is born in Palo Alto. He later attended Menlo-Atherton High and turns 77. (H)
8. **The Smiths:**
   - Final LP, *Strangeways, Here We Come*, released Sept. 28, 1987.
   - First gig, Oct. 4, 1982, at the Ritz in Manchester. (H)
