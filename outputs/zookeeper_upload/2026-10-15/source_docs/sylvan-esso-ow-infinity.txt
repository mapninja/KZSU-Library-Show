Album / Artist: Ow ∞ / Sylvan Esso

Label: Psychic Hotline

Release Date: September 11, 2026

Review Date: 10/8/2026

Reviewer: DJ Stace

General Comments / Reviews:

Electro-pop that wanders off into fuzz guitars, breakbeats and trip-hop, and comes back smiling like a Cheshire. Too many FCCs, but the clean ones are worth it. - DJ Stace

Release Notes:

Fifth album from Amelia Meath and Nick Sanborn; first in four years and first on their own label.

Built over four years at their Betty's studio in Chapel Hill, N.C., with more collaborators than before.

released September 11, 2026

FCCs: 3,6,8,11

RIYL: Flock of Dimes, Little Dragon, Purity Ring, Lucius

Play: All but FCCs, Favs Rated with up to \*\*\*\*\*

Tracklist:

1.  \*\*\* Entertainment & Coca-Cola 02:03 - Opens with a high-pitched wail that gives way to a soft melody that sounds like it’s coming from an antique toy recorder

1.  \*\*\*\* Flood Season 02:24 - Light, poppy with great beats. Bouncy with great melody, and vocals. Excellent.

1.  Hot Slob 02:45 - FCC fuck x3, bitch x1.Punked-up summer anthem. guitar by Jenn Wasner of Wye Oak. Worth a radio edit (there’s one on kzsustanford@gmail YTM account). Best song on the album.

1.  \*\*\*\* Concrete Glen 03:30 - Samples Sigur Ros' "Svefn-g-englar" Hazy. Slow and stochastic rhythms and soft bouncing “Nanny-boo-boo” lyrics. Excellent.

1.  \*\*\* Back Again 04:32 - Video game synth riffs and backbeat percussion pads, wonky clarinet bridge and soft lilting vocals.

1.  Ribbon 01:53 - FCC fuck x1. a cappella chorale with cursing.

1.  \*\*\* Sledge 02:48 - Fast-pased, driving rocker. Noisy distorted radio-type audio gives way to powerful vocals, distorted guitar and a repeated refrain.

1.  \*\*\*\* Right to Left 03:32 - FCC fuck x1. Great shuffling snare edge rhythm. Breathy and soft vocals.

1.  \*\*\*\* Good at Pain 02:48 - Great little weirdo wahwah organ vocal Intro. Create chaotic rhythms. Bouncy and surprisingly dancey. Excellent.

1.  \*\*\* Jessica 03:31 - More video game midi intro, with soft vocal intro.  Plucky and not at all melancholy. *About the duo's friend Jessica, who died of sudden liver failure in 2023* (Paste).

1.  \*\*\* On & On 04:43 - FCC shit x4. Warbly little electro keyboard acoustic guitar pluck intro. Speedy shuffling little guitar Almost Bossa Nova.

1.  \*\*\*\* Plastic Sword 03:01 - pseudo-bluegrass over-auto-tuned scat and rap. Strange and wonderful. Great closer.
