Release Date: October 2, 2026

Review Date: 10/8/2026

Reviewer: DJ Stace

General Comments / Reviews:

Posthumous Fall: unused Mark E. Smith vocals from 2001 to 2014, pieced into an “Exquisite Corpse” of nine tracks by Ed Blaney and Simon Archer, with some added vocals from the two of them. One more Fall album, with asterisks. - DJ Stace

Release Notes:

Recorded and assembled by Ed Blaney and Simon "Ding" Archer (a former Fall bassist and engineer, 6DB studio; also worked with PJ Harvey and Pixies).

Reports conflict on whether Smith's family and estate approved the release; his former manager Pamela Vander says she and Smith's sister did not.

9 tracks, about 44 minutes.

released October 2, 2026

FCCs: None found

RIYL: Wire, Gang of Four, Magazine, The Birthday Party, Pavement

Play: All, Favs Rated with up to \*\*\*\*\*

Tracklist:

1.  \*\*\*\* 30 Degrees 04:03 - Slow, Plodding, Driving. Smith’s spoken snarl is supreme.

1.  \*\*\* So Long 04:08 - Buzzing trancy, Vocals at turns Smith’s distorted or Blaney and Archer with clarity.

1.  \*\*\* Colonel's Retreat 05:03 - Nice slow, plodding drum, bass, distorted guitars (sounds a little Duane Denison of Jesus Lizard), gives way to driving rocker and back.

1.  \*\*\*\* A Caveat 04:10 - Military tattoo snare intro with intermittent power chords gives way to a jangling shoegaze rocker. Smith’s narrative snarly talking vocals are the star again.

1.  \*\*\* Final Position 05:32 - Mid-tempo, rhythm-shifting, buzzing palimpsest of vocals and saw-edge guitars accelerates to chaos and back to unintelligible vocal layer loops. Trancey and mesmerizing.

1.  \*\*\*\* Retro Song 04:10 - Up tempo rocker. Zipping distorted keyboard riffs and Driving beat. Growling Smith vocals and punk backup callbacks from Blaney and Archer.

1.  \*\*\* Irish Northern Man 07:03 - Slow plodding, quiet but noisey intro. Smith’s dominant narrative vocals over the top are startling. Over 7 minutes.

1.  \*\*\*\* Dehydrated 03:55 - More driving buzzing quasi-Fall goodness. Shoegazey and chunky and sparkly at turns. Excellent.

1.  \*\*\*\* The Book 06:23 - Slow plodding weird muffled rhythm gives way to highhat ticking-clock pace and atmospheric amped guitar. Layering Smith’s vocals to excellent effect. Moments of rhythmic complexity give way to the ticking again. Cool and satisfying.
