Album / Artist: You're Gonna Need A Little Music / Yard Act

Label: Republic Records

Release Date: July 17, 2026

Review Date: 10/8/2026

Reviewer: DJ Stace

General Comments / Reviews:

Yard Act trade some of the sneer for groove, and it works. Dance-punk you can actually dance to. Lots of FCCs, sadly. Still some VERY solid clean tracks- DJ Stace
