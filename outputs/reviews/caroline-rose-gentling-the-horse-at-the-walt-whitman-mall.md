Release Notes:

First album on Rose's own label, SUCK records, following Loner, Superstar, The Art of Forgetting (New West) and the Bandcamp-only year of the slug.
Written, arranged and produced by Rose; produced, engineered and mixed by John Congleton at Animal Rites Studio, Los Angeles; additional engineering by Rachel White; mastered by Matt Colton at Metropolis Studios, London.
Rose - vocals, guitar, keys, drum machine; Zac Rae - guitar, bass, keys; Carla Azar - drums, percussion; John Congleton - drum machine.
Production connections: Congleton has also produced St. Vincent, Sharon Van Etten and Angel Olsen. Azar is the drummer of Autolux and has played on Jack White's solo albums.
Press describes a hyperreal Americana fantasy where humor, memoir and suburban dread collide, with surf-rock and spaghetti-Western touches (press coverage; Consequence).

Tracklist:
1. Yip Yip Yow 02:46 - Mid to uptempo (~117 BPM). Slamming guitar riff, whooping drums, upbeat and loud (WERS). Press hears psychobilly, alt-country and garage rock.
2. Chow Mein 02:24 - Tempo sources disagree (Shazam says ~164 BPM, a critic calls it laid back). Quirky, laid-back and groovy with vocal harmonies and a polished DIY feel (If It's Too Loud). Menace and slapstick on an awkward night out (Consequence).
3. Valery (please don't be mad at me) 03:09 - FCC unverified: no lyrics posted.
4. Cement Head 02:11 - FCC unverified: no lyrics posted.
5. Goddamn Train 01:54 - Caution “goddamn” x2 (Genius posts no sections).
6. Notes to Teenage Self 03:45 - FCC unverified: no lyrics posted.
7. Conversation with Shiv (AKA liquid k song) 02:57 - FCC unverified: no lyrics posted.
8. Hello Again, Jim 03:26 - Fuzz-blasted lead riff, piston-pumping drums and a surfy bassline, with a defiant, snarky vocal (Rolling Stone; See Rock Live). About toxic music-industry men named Jim (Rose, Under the Radar).
9. Calm 03:14 - FCC “motherfucker” x3 (Chorus, once in each of three Choruses). Persistent distorted guitar riff, driving beat and striking percussion (She Makes Music; Teco Apple). Sarcastic, tongue-in-cheek vocal. Rose calls it a sardonic take on feeling mentally unwell (Under the Radar).
10. RIDE (!!!) 02:19 - FCC unverified: no lyrics posted.
11. Everything in its Right Place 04:07 - Title matches Radiohead's Kid A track but it is not listed as a cover. Rose has an earlier original of the same title on year of the slug (2025); unclear whether this is a new recording.
