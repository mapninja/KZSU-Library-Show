Release Notes:

PRE-RELEASE. Out Oct. 23, 2026 (not yet released as of Oct. 18) on SUCK records (catalog SUCK02), the imprint Rose launched this year. Consequence calls it Rose's seventh studio album. It follows year of the slug (Feb. 7, 2025, Bandcamp and vinyl only) and The Art of Forgetting (2023, New West).
11 tracks, 32:12 total per Qobuz. Written, arranged and produced by Rose; produced, engineered and mixed by John Congleton at Animal Rites Studio, Los Angeles; additional engineering by Rachel White; mastered by Matt Colton at Metropolis Studios, London.
Rose - vocals, guitar, keys, drum machine; Zac Rae - guitar, bass, keys; Carla Azar - drums, percussion; John Congleton - drum machine.
Singles: Yip Yip Yow (May 12, a studio version of a song Rose has played live for about a decade), Chow Mein (Aug. 11, with the album announcement), and Hello Again, Jim and Calm (released together Sept. 15). Rose is on a North American tour opening for Modest Mouse this fall.
Genius posts lyrics for six of the 11 tracks (1, 2, 5, 8, 9 and 11). Track 9 is FCC and track 5 is a caution word. Tracks 3, 4, 6, 7 and 10 have no lyrics posted yet and are unscreened.

Tracklist:
1. Yip Yip Yow 02:46 - Opener. First single (May 12). Studio version of a song Rose has played live for about a decade (Under the Radar).
2. Chow Mein 02:24 - Second single (Aug. 11, with the album announcement). Consequence calls it quirky, a collision of menace and slapstick on an awkward night out.
3. Valery (please don't be mad at me) 03:09 - FCC unverified: no lyrics posted.
4. Cement Head 02:11 - FCC unverified: no lyrics posted.
5. Goddamn Train 01:54 - Caution “goddamn” x2 (Genius posts no sections). Shortest track. Title contains the caution word. Marked explicit on Qobuz. A track of the same title and runtime is on year of the slug (Feb. 7, 2025).
6. Notes to Teenage Self 03:45 - FCC unverified: no lyrics posted.
7. Conversation with Shiv (AKA liquid k song) 02:57 - FCC unverified: no lyrics posted. A track titled "conversation with shiv (liquid k song)" (2:58) is on year of the slug (Feb. 7, 2025).
8. Hello Again, Jim 03:26 - Single (Sept. 15, with Calm). Video is a black-and-white live clip directed by Chloé Aktaş, with actor Jimmi Simpson as the bartender. Rose says the title character stands in for the many music-industry men named Jim (Under the Radar).
9. Calm 03:14 - FCC “motherfucker” x3 (Chorus, once in each of three Choruses). Single (Sept. 15, with Hello Again, Jim), released with an animated visualizer. Marked explicit on Qobuz. Rose calls it a sardonic take on feeling mentally unwell (Under the Radar).
10. RIDE (!!!) 02:19 - FCC unverified: no lyrics posted.
11. Everything in its Right Place 04:07 - Closer and longest track. Shares its title with the Radiohead song from Kid A (2000), but a track of this title (3:45) is on year of the slug (Feb. 7, 2025), where all songs are credited to Rose. Not listed as a cover.
