Release Notes:

New album from Brighton band Fujiya & Miyagi, out Oct. 30 (not yet released) on their own Impossible Objects of Desire imprint. It is their first major release in four years, after Slight Variations (Sept. 2022). That Eric Alper notes it arrives 20 years after their breakthrough album Transparent Things.
David Best - vocals, guitar; Steve Lewis - keyboards, backing vocals; Ben Adamo - bass, backing vocals; Ed Chivers - drums, backing vocals (Under the Radar).
Nine tracks per Bandcamp and every published tracklist. The last title is Manufacturing Delay on Bandcamp and Manufacturing Delays elsewhere. Clash says 10 songs including one instrumental, but no source lists a tenth title.
Made over four years of sessions on England's south coast; the press release says the plan to record it live was scaled back to a direct, melody-first approach. Producer, studio and mastering credits are not published. Themes are loss, fatherhood and ageing, and 158 is about the death of Best's friend and former band member Matthew Collins, aka Grunder (That Eric Alper).
Singles: Lowest Common Denominator (Aug. 11, with the album announcement), Point 33 Recurring (Sept. 10, per Dork) and No Green Light (Oct. 6). The Bandcamp pre-order streams these three now.
Lyrics are posted only for track 1, which screens clean. Tracks 2 and 6 have no lyrics on Genius or LRCLIB as of Oct. 18. Runtimes are published for three tracks only.
