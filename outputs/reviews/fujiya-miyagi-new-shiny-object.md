Release Notes:

David Best - vocals, guitar; Steve Lewis - keyboards, backing vocals; Ben Adamo - bass, backing vocals; Ed Chivers - drums, backing vocals (Under the Radar).
Producer, studio and mastering are not published. Made over four years of sessions on England's south coast; the plan to record it live was scaled back to a direct, melody-first approach (press release). Clash says one of the songs is an instrumental but does not say which.
Lineage: Best and Lewis started the band in Brighton in 1999. Matt Hainsby played bass on the breakthrough Transparent Things era; Adamo and Chivers joined by 2016. Albums include Transparent Things, Lightbulbs, Ventriloquizzing, Artificial Sweeteners and Slight Variations (Wikipedia, AllMusic). On their own Impossible Objects of Desire label after leaving Yep Roc.
Krautrock lineage (Neu!, CAN, Kraftwerk) with Talking Heads and New Order in the pop (Clash). Themes are loss, fatherhood and ageing; 158 is about the death of Best's friend and former band member Matthew Collins, aka Grunder (That Eric Alper).
