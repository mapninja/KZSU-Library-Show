Album / Artist: You're Gonna Need A Little Music / Yard Act

Label: Republic Records

Release Date: July 17, 2026
Review Date: 10/8/2026
Reviewer: DJ Stace

General Comments / Reviews:

Yard Act trade some of the sneer for groove, and it works. Dance-punk you can actually dance to. Lots of FCCs, sadly. - DJ Stace  [DRAFT, edit or replace]

Release Notes:

Leeds band's third album.
Produced by Justin Meldal-Johnsen (Beck, St. Vincent, Nine Inch Nails); finished at his Los Angeles studio.
Critics call it their most melodic and groove-driven set, with less of the earlier sneer.
Single Redeemer. NACC #41 the week of Oct. 1. Six of 11 tracks carry FCC words.
Recorded between Leeds and Los Angeles. No guest artists found (NME). Outlets differ on label: Republic (Under the Radar) or Island.
released July 17, 2026

FCCs: 1,3,6,7,8,9

RIYL: Fontaines D.C., LCD Soundsystem, The Streets, Parquet Courts

Play: All but FCCs, Favs Rated with up to *****

Tracklist:
1. Empty Pledges 04:25 - FCC fuck x1, shit x1. Opener; single with official and lyric videos. Treble calls it the record's full-fledged rap moment.
2. New Beginnings 03:28 - Second single with video, June 23, 2026 (Stereogum). Rolling Stone cites muscular guitars and a shout-along chorus.
3. Tall Tales 03:52 - FCC fuck x1. NME cites a wonky piano.
4. Fiction 02:41 - NME calls its hook repetitive. Producer ran a drum machine through the vocals (Apple Music). Line of Best Fit: vocals battle Sam Shipstone's crunching riffs.
5. You're Gonna Need A Little Music 04:41 - NME cites a euphoric chorus. Rolling Stone: it glides where New Beginnings surges. Lyric video on YouTube.
6. Cherophobe Rock 02:53 - FCC shit x1. Some reviewers call it the generic one. Title nods to cherophobe, fear of happiness (Treble). DIY notes crunching vocals.
7. Thrill Of The Chase 02:31 - FCC fuck x1. Apple Music: a drum 'n' bass groove slowed down, with live drums and James Brown-style guitars. Rolling Stone calls it arguably the strongest track.
8. Janey Said 04:51 - FCC fuck x1, shit x1. Apple Music calls it the heart of the album. Mojo calls it a mournful Streets shuffle.
9. Redeemer 03:59 - FCC fuck x1, shit x2. Lead single with video; announced the album May 7, 2026 (Our Culture). BrooklynVegan calls it smouldering and bluesy, the most rock Yard Act have sounded.
10. Talky Talky People 03:56 - DIY compares the mood to a Blur B-side.
11. Over The Barrel 04:33 - Barroom piano and a big indie-rock chorus, ending in a rush of noise (New Noise).
