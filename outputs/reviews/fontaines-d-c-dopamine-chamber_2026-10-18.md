Release Notes:

Fifth album from the Dublin band, after Romance (2024). Produced by James Ford, who took over from Dan Carey (producer of the first three albums) on Romance.
Ford is also a member of Simian Mobile Disco and The Last Shadow Puppets, and has produced Arctic Monkeys and Depeche Mode (Memento Mori). Marta Salogni engineered; she also recorded and mixed Memento Mori and has worked with Björk and Bon Iver. Matt Colton mastered.
Recorded mostly at Decoy Studios in Suffolk, plus Narcissus Studios in London. Grian Chatten cut his vocals separately with Ford in Palermo, Sicily.
Grian Chatten - vocals, guitar; Carlos O'Connell - guitar, synths; Conor Curley - guitar; Conor Deegan - bass; Tom Coll - drums. Rosalía guests on Where Is Gone?, the band's first guest vocalist. Strings arranged by Chatten, Richard Jones, Laura Moody and Elysian Collective.
About the doomscroll and phone-as-painkiller culture, technological dependence and collapse. Chatten says he left the hope out on purpose (Rolling Stone Philippines).

Tracklist:
1. Six Shot Morning 04:56 - Uptempo (~131 BPM). Synth intro, then a heavy bassline and a cool, commanding male lead vocal (Rolling Stone Philippines). Mechanical Depeche Mode death march (NME); gated, grainy drums (The Soundboard).
2. Marianne 03:45 - Slow to midtempo (~82 BPM). Ballad with gauzy synths and spiralling strings (The Soundboard, Guardian). Cyborg-meets-symphony balladry (NME); Moroder-style electronic tone with a Duran Duran feel (Beats Per Minute).
3. Mimesis 03:51 - Mid to uptempo (~128 BPM). Dominant bass, sharp snare hits and cold synths (Beats Per Minute). New Order-style bassline (Guardian); 80s dance-pop feel (Rolling Stone Philippines).
4. Happy For You 03:48 - Uptempo (~130 BPM). Flanged, Cure-like guitar twang (Guardian, Rolling Stone Philippines), building to a massive guitar drop (AllMusic, The Soundboard). Bitter, sarcastic song about a breakup (Beats Per Minute).
5. Demolition 03:19 - Fast (~176 BPM). Sweeping open guitars and crunching synths into a big chorus (The Soundboard). Drum machine pulse (Rolling Stone Philippines); grunge attack (Guardian); Smiths-like guitar lead (Beats Per Minute).
6. Tongue 03:13 - FCC “shit” x10 (Refrain). Tempo check: songbpm says ~165 BPM, but critics hear trip-hop beats, so it may be half-time. Slinky bassline and piano (Rolling Stone Philippines); hollowed, heavy mix with a piano interlude (The Soundboard) and a jazzy bridge (Beats Per Minute). Trip-hop gloom (NME). Listed in FCC Edit Needed.
7. One Man 04:58 - Tempo check: songbpm says ~132 BPM, but critics call it a ballad, so it may be half-time. Epic, string-drenched romantic ballad that builds to a big finish (Beats Per Minute). Breathy and elegiac (NME).
8. Where Is Gone? (with ROSALÍA) 05:16 - Slow to midtempo (~80 BPM). Features Rosalía: male and female vocals together on an orchestral duet. Restrained strings, alt-country feel (The Soundboard); Morricone-style ballad (NME, Guardian). No FCC words in the English lyrics posted; Spanish checked for common swear words.
9. You Miss Me 03:35 - Midtempo (~101 BPM). Smoky guitar hook (Rolling Stone Philippines). Edgy synth strings and sharp beats in the verses, string-led crooner chorus (Beats Per Minute); Robert Smith-style verse (NME).
10. Japan 03:14 - FCC “fuck” x3, “shit” x3 (Chorus). Uptempo (~148 BPM). Bittersweet acoustic singalong (NME). Dream-pop feel, Beach House meets a-ha (Beats Per Minute).
11. Intimate 03:57 - Uptempo (~133 BPM). Skittering sci-fi hymn (NME). Hi-hat-driven headbanger of a closer (Rolling Stone Philippines).
