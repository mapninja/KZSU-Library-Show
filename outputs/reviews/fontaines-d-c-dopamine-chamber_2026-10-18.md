Release Notes:

Fifth album from the Dublin band, after Romance (2024). Produced by James Ford, who also made Romance. Metacritic score 85 from 19 reviews.
Most of it was recorded at Decoy Studios in Suffolk, with additional sessions at Narcissus Studios in London (February 2025 to March 2026). Grian Chatten recorded his vocals separately with Ford in Palermo, Sicily. Marta Salogni engineered; Matt Colton mastered (Wikipedia).
Grian Chatten - vocals, guitar; Carlos O'Connell - guitar, synths; Conor Curley - guitar; Conor Deegan - bass; Tom Coll - drums.
Rosalía guests on Where Is Gone?, the band's first guest vocalist on a record. Strings arranged by Chatten, Richard Jones, Laura Moody and Elysian Collective.
Singles: Marianne (Aug. 18), Tongue (Sept. 30) and Where Is Gone? (Oct. 12). 11 tracks, about 44 minutes. Released Oct. 16, 2026.
Reviews (Guardian, NME, Pitchfork, AllMusic) hear synths, strings and electronics over guitars, with comparisons to The Cure, Depeche Mode, New Order and Massive Attack.

Tracklist:
1. Six Shot Morning 04:56 - Opener. Teased Aug. 15; live debut in Cádiz in August. Mechanical Depeche Mode death march (NME); chugging bassline and searching synths (AllMusic).
2. Marianne 03:45 - Lead single (Aug. 18), with a Dave Meyers video. Live debut in Cádiz in August. Strings arranged by Chatten, Richard Jones, Laura Moody and Elysian Collective. Cyborg-meets-symphony balladry (NME); spiralling strings (Guardian).
3. Mimesis 03:51 - Synth-pop noir (NME); a New Order-style bassline (Guardian).
4. Happy For You 03:48 - Debuted live at Leeds Festival, Aug. 29. A Cure-like guitar twang (Guardian); a massive guitar drop (AllMusic).
5. Demolition 03:19 - Album highlight, with fuzzy electronica (NME); a grunge attack (Guardian).
6. Tongue 03:13 - FCC “shit” x10 (Refrain). Second single (Sept. 30), with a New York-shot video directed by PHC (John Angus Stewart). In FCC Edit Needed. Trip-hop gloom (NME); the oddest track on the album (AllMusic).
7. One Man 04:58 - Second-longest track. Breathy and elegiac (NME); starts the album's run of tender songs (AllMusic).
8. Where Is Gone? (with ROSALÍA) 05:16 - Features Rosalía, the band's first guest vocalist. Third single (Oct. 12). Longest track. A Morricone-style ballad and would-be Bond theme (NME, Guardian). No FCC words in the English lyrics posted; Spanish checked for common swear words.
9. You Miss Me 03:35 - Robert Smith-style verse (NME).
10. Japan 03:14 - FCC “fuck” x3, “shit” x3 (Chorus). Bittersweet acoustic singalong (NME). Bandcamp lists 3:14; Wikipedia lists 3:15.
11. Intimate 03:57 - Closer. Skittering sci-fi hymn (NME).
