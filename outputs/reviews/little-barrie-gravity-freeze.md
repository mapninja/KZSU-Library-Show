Release Notes:

Sixth album from the London trio led by guitarist Barrie Cadogan; new drummer Coote joins.
Recorded at Rat Salad Studios in Hornsey, North London, with Rupert Lyddon engineering and co-producing.
Reviewers hear a more restrained record after two albums with drummer Malcolm Catto: low-slung grooves and bursts of guitar.
Singles More Bad Miles of Road, It Isn't Soul and Luggin' Hurt. Several tracks have no lyrics posted; listen first.
