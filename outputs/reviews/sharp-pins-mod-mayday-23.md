General Comments / Reviews:

Kai Slater's teenage bedroom cassette, cleaned up for the rest of us. Kinks and early Who by way of a Chicago basement. Fuzzy, catchy, over in a flash. - DJ Stace  [DRAFT, edit or replace]

Release Notes:

Reissue of the 11-track cassette Chicago's Kai Slater (also of Lifeguard and Dwaal Troupe) self-released as a teenager in 2023, about 50 hand-dubbed copies.
Covers and rough recordings, remastered; out on Perennial / K Records (LP/CD, cat. PRNL071/KLP320).
Released with a new single, Saturday Sun. NACC #15 the week of Oct. 1.
Reviewers compare it to The Kinks, early Who and Guided By Voices.
Title nods to Mods Mayday '79, a compilation from a London show with Secret Affair, Beggar, The Mods and Squire (Why Now).
