Release Notes:

Original score by the Milan instrumental quartet for Francesco Zippel's documentary Ellroy vs L.A. (Quoiat Films), a portrait of author James Ellroy and Los Angeles that includes footage of the band recording. The film won a 2026 Nastro d'Argento. Rockit counts it as the band's eighth soundtrack.
Enrico Gabrielli - piano, keyboards, sax, flute, bass clarinet; Massimo Martellotta - electric guitar, sitar guitar, synthesizer, organ; Fabio Rondanini - drums, percussion; Roberto Dragonetti - bass guitar.
Produced and mixed by Tommaso Colliva. Recorded by Colliva and Simone Bertorello at Laboratori Testone (Milan); mastered by Giovanni Versari at La Maestà (Milan).
Singles: Riots (May 13, 2026), L.A. Confidential (June 23) and Vacation and Probation (Sept. 9). Album announced May 13 on Record Kicks. 12 tracks, about 40 minutes; vinyl is a limited run of 1,200 black LPs, plus a CD digipack.
Not to be confused with a four-track "LSD on Sunset Blvd" EP that shows up on third-party download sites. It lists tracks that match this album (LSD on Sunset Blvd, Vacation and Probation, L.A. Confidential, Riots), and no official EP appears on Bandcamp or Record Kicks. The title track is track 2 here.
Reception: OndaRock 7/10; Rockit named it album of the week. No English-language press review found.

Tracklist:
1. Black Dahlia 03:17 - Instrumental. Opener; Bandcamp lists a video. Named for Ellroy's 1987 novel. Rockit hears a reference to the opening of Brian De Palma's 2006 film of the book.
2. LSD on Sunset Blvd 03:36 - Instrumental. Title track of the separate EP listed on download sites (see release notes); unofficial.
3. Pacific Safari 03:46 - Instrumental. OndaRock singles out its hypnotic psychedelia.
4. The Big Nowhere part 1 04:01 - Instrumental. Named for Ellroy's 1988 novel; first of two parts. Rockit and OndaRock both call the two parts smoky and nocturnal.
5. L.A. Confidential 04:35 - Instrumental. Second single (June 23, 2026). Named for Ellroy's 1990 novel. Longest track.
6. Riots 02:37 - Instrumental. First single (May 13, 2026). OndaRock hears funk close to the band's early cop-movie work.
7. Chalet Manson 04:03 - Instrumental. Rockit hears a nod to Franco Battiato's song "Shock in My Town".
8. The Big Nowhere part 2 02:41 - Instrumental. Second of two parts.
9. Vacation and Probation 02:34 - Instrumental. Third single (Sept. 9, 2026); Bandcamp lists a video. The band calls it one of the most overtly cinematic tracks on the score. OndaRock praises it.
10. White Jazz 02:55 - Instrumental. Named for Ellroy's 1992 novel. Rockit notes it opens on brushed drums.
11. Feels Like Home 04:08 - Instrumental.
12. Moonlight Sonate 02:18 - Instrumental. Closer and shortest track. Rockit identifies it as a version of Beethoven's Moonlight Sonata (original composer: Ludwig van Beethoven).
