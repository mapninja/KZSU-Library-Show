Release Notes:

Star Moles is Emily Moales, a New Hampshire native based in Philadelphia, releasing music since 2017 (earlier albums include Camelot, Here Lies Captain Joy, Three Chimes, At Silent Palace! and Snack Monster, a medieval-courtly-love folk concept record). This one trades the fantasy and literature for everyday life; her liner notes call it purgatorio (Paste). Not the AC/DC album, no covers.
Emily Moales - acoustic guitar, drums, bass, piano, vocals, percussion; Kevin Basko - bass, saxophone, backing vocals, percussion; Sam Sullivan - electric guitar (tracks 1, 4, 5, 10); Jem Seidel - percussion (track 9). Mostly piano, acoustic guitar and hand drums, lo-fi (Turn and Work). Cover art by Matthew Reed (TV Beaches).
Produced, recorded, mixed and mastered by Kevin Basko (Rubber Band Gun) at his Historic New Jersey studio in South Philadelphia. Basko, a Berklee grad who has toured with Foxygen, has worked with Moales since they met online in 2017 and plays with her in the duo Hot Machine (Philadelphia Inquirer).
Philadelphia scene: Historic New Jersey is a small South Philly studio, label and collective (Star Moles, Rubber Band Gun, folk-pop siblings Sam and Louise Sullivan, drummer Jem Seidel) whose members play on each other's records (Inquirer).
Dead Oceans (Secretly Group, Bloomington, Indiana; also home to Mitski, Phoebe Bridgers and Slowdive) took the record on for its vinyl release.

Tracklist:
1. The End 02:28 - Slow to midtempo (~80 BPM). Piano riff reminiscent of Fatboy Slim's "Praise You" (Paste). Lo-fi, with a charming false start on the second verse (Turn and Work). Electric guitar: Sam Sullivan. Shortest track.
2. Real Magic 03:56 - Uptempo (~152 BPM).
3. Time 03:23 - Mid to uptempo (~128 BPM). Undulating guitar and percussion lines settle into an icy groove (Stereogum). Catchy lo-fi indie (Turn and Work).
4. Overdog 03:56 - Midtempo (~113 BPM). Bright piano ballad at a midtempo pace (Turn and Work, Stereogum). Electric guitar: Sam Sullivan.
5. Day Off 02:59 - Midtempo (~107 BPM). Electric guitar: Sam Sullivan.
6. Factory Train 04:00 - Uptempo (~142 BPM).
7. Spinning 04:17 - Pace: check (source says ~142 BPM, but Paste calls it a ballad). Dreamy country ballad (Paste).
8. Control Freak 05:20 - Pace: check (source says ~135 BPM, but Paste calls it the album's slowest song). Piano ballad, the longest and slowest track, with the vocal dropping to a gravelly low register at the peak (Paste).
9. Skip the Party 03:16 - Pace: check (source says ~75 BPM, but Paste calls it jaunty). Jaunty (Paste). Percussion: Jem Seidel.
10. Halo 04:03 - Fast (~162 BPM). Closer. Electric guitar: Sam Sullivan.
