Release Notes:

Star Moles is Emily Moales, a Philadelphia songwriter (originally from New Hampshire) who has been releasing music since 2017. Not related to the AC/DC album of the same name, and no covers: all songs written by Moales.
Produced, recorded, mixed and mastered by Kevin Basko (Rubber Band Gun) at his Historic New Jersey studio in Philadelphia, 2025.
Emily Moales - acoustic guitar, drums, bass, piano, vocals, percussion; Kevin Basko - bass, saxophone, backing vocals, percussion; Sam Sullivan - electric guitar (tracks 1, 4, 5, 10); Jem Seidel - percussion (track 9). Cover art by Matthew Reed (TV Beaches).
10 tracks, about 38 minutes. Follows the 2025 releases Snack Monster (Aug.) and At Night (Oct.). Turn and Work calls Time the focus track and Overdog an advance single.
On Oct. 7, 2026 Moales signed to Dead Oceans, which will issue Highway to Hell on vinyl for the first time on Dec. 4. A new single, Born Again (video by Lucky Marvel), came out the same day and is the 11th track on the Bandcamp edition. It is not on the 10-track streaming album.
Dead Oceans announced a January 2027 headline tour. Lyrics are posted for all 10 tracks, and none has FCC words.
