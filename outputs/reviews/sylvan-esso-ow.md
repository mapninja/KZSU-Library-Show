Release Notes:

Fifth album from Amelia Meath and Nick Sanborn; first in four years and first on their own label.
Built over four years at their Betty's studio in Chapel Hill, N.C., with more collaborators than before.
Adds distorted guitars, breakbeats, trip-hop and found sound to their electronic pop.
Lead single Concrete Glen; also Hot Slob and Flood Season. NACC #7 the week of Oct. 1. Four tracks carry FCC words, including On & On (shit x4).
