Album / Artist: Ow ∞ / Sylvan Esso

Label: Psychic Hotline

Release Date: September 11, 2026
Review Date: 10/8/2026
Reviewer: DJ Stace

General Comments / Reviews:

Electro-pop that wanders off into fuzz guitars, breakbeats and trip-hop, and comes back grinning. Too many FCCs, but the clean ones are worth it. - DJ Stace  [DRAFT, edit or replace]

Release Notes:

Fifth album from Amelia Meath and Nick Sanborn; first in four years and first on their own label.
Built over four years at their Betty's studio in Chapel Hill, N.C., with more collaborators than before.
Adds distorted guitars, breakbeats, trip-hop and found sound to their electronic pop.
Lead single Concrete Glen; also Hot Slob and Flood Season. NACC #7 the week of Oct. 1. Four tracks carry FCC words, including On & On (shit x4).
Label not in the Zookeeper label table.
Written over about four years. Bandcamp says six different drummers play on it (via WUNC).
released September 11, 2026

FCCs: 3,6,8,11

RIYL: Flock of Dimes, Little Dragon, Purity Ring, Lucius

Play: All but FCCs, Favs Rated with up to *****

Tracklist:
1. Entertainment & Coca-Cola 02:03 - Opens with a high-pitched wail that gives way to a soft melody (Plus One).
2. Flood Season 02:24 - Single with video. Reviewers read it as about a new climate normal or ongoing struggle (Song Bar, Treble).
3. Hot Slob 02:45 - FCC fuck x3, bitch x1. Single with video; described as a punked-up summer anthem. Released June 10, 2026 (Shore Fire Media). Stereogum calls the guitar-heavy sound a surprising new direction. Song Bar hears 1990s alt rock, glam guitar and electro-pop. Drums by TJ Maiani, guitar by Jenn Wasner of Wye Oak (verify at Shore Fire or FLOOD).
4. Concrete Glen 03:30 - Lead single (July 21, 2026) with video by Aaron Anderson and Eric Timothy Carlson; samples Sigur Ros' "Svefn-g-englar" (Agaetis Byrjun, 1999) (mxdwn). Consequence calls it hazy electronic pop inspired by a surreal night out.
5. Back Again 04:32 - Video game synth riffs and big percussion pads (Treble). Wonky clarinet bridge (Spectrum Culture).
6. Ribbon 01:53 - FCC fuck x1. Includes an a cappella chorale (Treble).
7. Sledge 02:48 - Distorted guitar and a repeated chant refrain (Treble).
8. Right to Left 03:32 - FCC fuck x1. Spectrum Culture hears peace found in one's own collapse.
9. Good at Pain 02:48
10. Jessica 03:31 - About the duo's friend Jessica, who died of sudden liver failure in 2023 (Paste).
11. On & On 04:43 - FCC shit x4. Grew from a seedling in the band's session files (Shore Fire press).
12. Plastic Sword 03:01 - Scat-style pseudo-bluegrass syllables (Treble).
