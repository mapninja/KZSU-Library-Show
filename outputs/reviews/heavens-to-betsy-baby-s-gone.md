Release Notes:

NOT a new studio album. baby's gone is a 16-track collection of early recordings by the Olympia, Wash. riot grrrl duo, billed as their first release since 1994. Their only full-length, Calculated (March 1994), is not on it. The band styles its name lowercase; spelled Betsy, not Betsey. Kill Rock Stars catalog number KRS857.
Tracks 1 to 8: a previously unreleased demo tape engineered by Molly Neuman of Bratmobile at Evergreen State College, Olympia, Feb. 1992 (Bandcamp credits; the label's page calls it a 1991 tape). Tracks 9 to 12: Dec. 1992 session at the Red House, Olympia, with Neuman and Tim Green (the These Monsters Are Real recordings, 1993). Tracks 13 to 16: May 1994 session at House of Rock, Portland, with Donna Dresch and Jody Bleyle (the Direction 7-inch, 1994).
Corin Tucker - guitar, vocals (also Sleater-Kinney); Tracy Sawyer - drums (also The Husbands, The Flying Tigers, The Lies). Remastered in 2026 by Sarah Register; liner notes by Neuman. About 39 minutes in all.
Lead single "ain't never goin' back" (Aug. 19, with the announcement), with a lyric video by Painfully Optimistic Creative Projects. The Line of Best Fit calls them the last of Olympia's three original riot grrrl bands.
The duo played a secret Portland show this summer, their first in 32 years (Consequence), then announced a fall tour. Bay Area: Bottom of the Hill, San Francisco, Oct. 17 (Consequence) and Oct. 18 (Bandcamp). Olympia Oct. 24, Portland Oct. 28.
Lyrics are posted for all 16 tracks. Five have FCC words: 2, 9, 10, 11 and 14. No caution words ("bitch" and similar) turned up in any track.

Tracklist:
1. good food 02:25 - Opener. From the Feb. 1992 demo tape. The Line of Best Fit pairs it with Bikini Kill's "Feels Blind".
2. factory 01:42 - FCC “fucking” x1 (midway through; Genius posts no sections). From the Feb. 1992 demo tape. The Line of Best Fit hears an echo of Pink Floyd's "Another Brick in the Wall (Part 2)" and calls it a short scourge of capitalism.
3. seek and hide 02:09 - From the Feb. 1992 demo tape. Genius titles it "Seek & Hide".
4. stay dead 02:23 - From the Feb. 1992 demo tape.
5. my red self 03:48 - Longest track. From the Feb. 1992 demo tape. Some outlets (Consequence, Punknews) print the title as "my red shelf"; Bandcamp and Kill Rock Stars say "my red self".
6. ain't never goin' back 01:46 - Lead single (Aug. 19), with a lyric video. From the Feb. 1992 demo tape and never released before. Consequence, relaying the press release, describes it as lo-fi garage rock.
7. baby's gone 02:24 - Title track. From the Feb. 1992 demo tape.
8. my secret 02:09 - Last track of the Feb. 1992 demo tape.
9. me & her 02:41 - FCC “fucking” x1 (Verse 2). From the Dec. 1992 session (These Monsters Are Real era). Genius titles it "Me & Her".
10. monsters 01:46 - FCC “fuck” x1 (Verse 2). From the Dec. 1992 session (These Monsters Are Real era). A separate "Monsters" from the 1993 release (1:49) also sits on the To Review playlist; it appears to be a stray duplicate of this song.
11. playground 02:47 - FCC “fucking” x2 (Chorus, in two of the three chorus repeats as posted). From the Dec. 1992 session (These Monsters Are Real era).
12. firefly 02:53 - From the Dec. 1992 session (These Monsters Are Real era). Named the favorite track by The Line of Best Fit's interviewer.
13. direction 03:01 - From the May 1994 session (Direction 7-inch). Title track of that 7-inch.
14. get out of my head 02:15 - FCC “fuck” x1 (late in song; Genius posts no sections). From the May 1994 session (Direction 7-inch).
15. the ones 02:26 - From the May 1994 session (Direction 7-inch).
16. driving song 02:14 - Closer. From the May 1994 session (Direction 7-inch).
