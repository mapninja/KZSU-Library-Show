Release Notes:

NOT a new studio album. A 16-track collection of early recordings by the Olympia, Wash. riot grrrl duo, billed as their first release since 1994 (their only full-length, Calculated, is not on it). Lo-fi throughout: the demo tape was recorded on a Tascam borrowed from the Evergreen AV library (Consequence). The band says the songs deal with sexual abuse, bodily autonomy and betrayal (press release via Tinnitist). The band styles its name lowercase.
Corin Tucker - guitar, vocals (later Sleater-Kinney, Filthy Friends and the Corin Tucker Band); Tracy Sawyer - drums (later The Husbands, The Flying Tigers, The Lies). The Line of Best Fit calls them the last of Olympia's three original riot grrrl bands, after Bikini Kill and Bratmobile.
Tracks 1 to 8: Feb. 1992 demo tape (Bandcamp credits; the label's page calls it a 1991 tape) engineered by Molly Neuman of Bratmobile (also the Frumpies and the PeeChees) at Evergreen State College, Olympia, who also wrote the liner notes. Tracks 9 to 12: Dec. 1992 session at the Red House, Olympia, with Neuman and Tim Green (Nation of Ulysses, later Louder Studios), the These Monsters Are Real recordings (1993). Tracks 13 to 16: May 1994 at House of Rock, Portland, engineered by Donna Dresch and Jody Bleyle, both of Team Dresch (Bleyle also runs Candy Ass Records), the Direction recordings (1994).
Remastered in 2026 by Sarah Register, formerly half of Talk Normal. Kill Rock Stars is the Olympia label that also released Bratmobile and Sleater-Kinney.

Tracklist:
1. good food 02:25 - Uptempo (~134 BPM). Lo-fi 1992 demo. Raw guitar and steady pulsing drums, few cymbals or fills (AllMusic). Sits alongside Bikini Kill's "Feels Blind" (Line of Best Fit).
2. factory 01:42 - FCC “fucking” x1 (midway through; Genius posts no sections). Pace: check (BPM source says ~118 BPM, but a critic hears a slow rumble). Lo-fi 1992 demo, short. Slow rumbling bass and vocals, with an echo of Pink Floyd's "Another Brick in the Wall (Part 2)" (Line of Best Fit).
3. seek and hide 02:09 - Midtempo (~107 BPM). Lo-fi 1992 demo. Raw guitar and steady pulsing drums, few cymbals or fills (AllMusic).
4. stay dead 02:23 - Mid to uptempo (~128 BPM). Lo-fi 1992 demo. Skeletal, little more than a few dissonant riffs and a timekeeping beat (AllMusic).
5. my red self 03:48 - Midtempo (~95 BPM). Lo-fi 1992 demo, longest track. Some outlets print the title as "my red shelf".
6. ain't never goin' back 01:46 - Uptempo (~130 BPM). Lo-fi 1992 demo, never released before. Raw, thrumming lo-fi garage rock (Consequence).
7. baby's gone 02:24 - Pace: check (BPM source says ~200 BPM, possibly double-time). Lo-fi 1992 demo. Raw guitar and steady pulsing drums, few cymbals or fills (AllMusic).
8. my secret 02:09 - Uptempo (~133 BPM). Lo-fi 1992 demo. Vocal reads as both threat and catharsis; the subject is sexual abuse (AllMusic).
9. me & her 02:41 - FCC “fucking” x1 (Verse 2). Uptempo (~135 BPM). Dec. 1992 session. Still raw, but slightly cleaner tones than the demos (AllMusic).
10. monsters 01:46 - FCC “fuck” x1 (Verse 2). Midtempo (~112 BPM). Dec. 1992 session, slightly cleaner tones than the demos (AllMusic). A separate "Monsters" from the 1993 release (1:49) also sits on the To Review playlist; it appears to be a stray duplicate of this song.
11. playground 02:47 - FCC “fucking” x2 (Chorus, in two of the three chorus repeats as posted). Uptempo (~131 BPM). Dec. 1992 session, slightly cleaner tones than the demos (AllMusic).
12. firefly 02:53 - Uptempo (~139 BPM). Dec. 1992 session, slightly cleaner tones than the demos (AllMusic). Opens with pleasant chords and a jangly drum, vocal emerging a little shyly (Line of Best Fit).
13. direction 03:01 - Midtempo (~107 BPM). May 1994 session. Multi-tracked guitars and bigger drum sound, a preview of early Sleater-Kinney (AllMusic).
14. get out of my head 02:15 - FCC “fuck” x1 (late in song; Genius posts no sections). Fast (~155 BPM). May 1994 session. Multi-tracked guitars and bigger drum sound, a preview of early Sleater-Kinney (AllMusic).
15. the ones 02:26 - Uptempo (~133 BPM). May 1994 session. Multi-tracked guitars and bigger drum sound, a preview of early Sleater-Kinney (AllMusic).
16. driving song 02:14 - Fast (~155 BPM). May 1994 session, closer. Multi-tracked guitars and bigger drum sound, a preview of early Sleater-Kinney (AllMusic).
