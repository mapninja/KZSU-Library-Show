Label: Fire Records

General Comments / Reviews:

Live art-punk, noise-rock and psych-blues from Melbourne, eight songs from a three-night TV Eye run in New York, plus a Saints cover. The band name is itself an FCC word, so announce them as TFS (the band's own shorthand), and tracks 1, 2 and 7 are FCC as well. - DJ Stace  [DRAFT, edit or replace]

Release Notes:

Second live album from the Melbourne band, after Inflatable Graveyard (Sept. 27, 2024, Three Lobed Recordings, recorded October 2022). Out Oct. 16, 2026 on Fire Records (FIRE842) as the follow-up to Fairyland Codex (June 20, 2025). Eight tracks, about 42 minutes.
Recorded over three nights of a sold-out residency at TV Eye in New York (Fire Records). Lineup: Gareth Liddiard - guitar, vocals; Fiona Kitschin - bass, vocals; Erica Dunn - guitar, keyboards, vocals; Lauren Hammel - drums.
Five songs come from Fairyland Codex (1, 2, 3, 4 and 6). Track 5 is from Braindrops (2019), track 7 is from A Laughing Death in Meatspace (2018), and track 8 is a cover of The Saints.
Previews: Stepping on a Rake (Aug. 13, with the album announcement) and Moscovium (Sept. 17). The shelf did not name a lead track; Fire Records led the announcement with Stepping on a Rake.
The band name is an FCC word. Bandcamp styles the artist as TFS and Fire Records copy writes Tropical Fu*k Storm. The band guest curates Le Guess Who in Utrecht (Nov. 5 to 8) and plays UK and Australian dates this fall and winter, with no Bay Area date listed.
Lyrics are posted on Genius for the studio versions of all eight songs, but Genius lists the live versions as unreleased. The screen below uses the studio lyrics, so ad-libs and stage banter are not covered. Three tracks have FCC words: 1, 2 and 7.

Tracklist:
1. Irukandji Syndrome 05:28 - FCC “shit” x1 (Verse 4, studio lyrics). Opener. From Fairyland Codex, where it is also track 1. Fire Records says the live version becomes a launchpad for a guitar solo in the manner of the Velvet Underground's "I Heard Her Call My Name".
2. Bloodsport 03:36 - FCC “cunts” x1 (late in song, about line 35 of 48; Genius posts no sections; studio lyrics). Shortest track. From Fairyland Codex (track 7).
3. Goon Show 05:52 - Caution “assholes” x1 (early in song, about line 6 of 40; Genius posts no sections; studio lyrics). From Fairyland Codex, where it was a single. The Needle Drop called the studio version a long, patient march with a wild guitar solo.
4. Stepping on a Rake 04:39 - First preview from the live album (Aug. 13). From Fairyland Codex. Fire Records calls it a ballad that takes on heavier undertones live. Screened on studio lyrics.
5. Who’s My Eugene? 06:05 - From Braindrops (2019), where it is track 3; released as a single July 26, 2019. Screened on studio lyrics.
6. Moscovium 07:14 - Longest track. Second preview from the live album (Sept. 17). Closes Fairyland Codex; Fire Records says it sounds more urgent live. Screened on studio lyrics.
7. You Let My Tyres Down 05:20 - FCC “shit” x1 (Verse 2, studio lyrics). From the debut A Laughing Death in Meatspace (2018); released as a single Feb. 2, 2018 (b/w "Back to the Wall").
8. This Perfect Day 03:37 - Closer. Cover of The Saints' 1977 single "(This) Perfect Day", written by Chris Bailey and Ed Kuepper. Screened on The Saints' lyrics.
