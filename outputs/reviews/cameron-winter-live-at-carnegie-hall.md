Release Notes:

First live album from Cameron Winter, the Geese frontman, recorded Dec. 11, 2025 in the Stern Auditorium at Carnegie Hall in New York. Solo set: Winter on vocals and a Steinway grand piano (Wikipedia, Paste).
12 tracks, about 67 minutes, on 2xLP, CD and digital (Bandcamp). Announced Aug. 19, 2026. Produced and mixed by Ariel Rechtshaid, mixed at Heavy Duty Studios in Burbank; mastered by Heba Kadry (liner notes via Wikipedia).
Studio vs. live: seven songs come from his 2024 debut Heavy Metal (tracks 2, 4, 5, 6, 7, 9, 10) and two from the Oct. 22, 2024 Singles EP (tracks 11, 12). Three are new and previously unreleased (tracks 1, 3, 8). Heavy Metal songs not played: Nausicaä (Love Will Be Revealed), We’re Thinking the Same Thing, Can’t Keep Anything.
Every returning song runs longer live than on record, by 45 seconds to over two minutes. Paste notes the French horn on the studio Drinking Age is replaced by piano, and that Cancer of the Skull and $0 are extended.
The concert was also filmed by Paul Thomas Anderson (with Benny Safdie as camera operator) on 35mm. Cameron Winter at Carnegie Hall premiered at the New York Film Festival on Oct. 1, 2026, with a U.S. release Nov. 20.
Lyrics are posted on Genius for every track, on both the Live at Carnegie Hall page and the plain song page. Screened both; FCC words match on each except track 3 (see track note).

Tracklist:
1. It All Fell in the River 04:30 - New, unreleased song (not on Heavy Metal). Opens the concert (Paste).
2. Try as I May 05:35 - FCC “shitless” x1 (Verse 2); caution “bitching” x1 (Verse 7). From Heavy Metal (studio 4:47).
3. Emperor XIII in Shades 04:18 - FCC “fucking” x1 (Verse 1.5 on the plain Genius page; the Live at Carnegie Hall page lacks it, so confirm by ear). New, unreleased song (not on Heavy Metal). Paste calls it an extension of his religious allusions.
4. The Rolling Stones 04:25 - Caution “goddamn” x1 (Verse 3). From Heavy Metal (studio 3:18, album opener). Paste says the guitar intro becomes a piano melody.
5. Love Takes Miles 04:13 - From Heavy Metal (studio 3:18). Paste hears a more urgent new arrangement.
6. Drinking Age 05:10 - FCC “shit” x1 (Genius posts no section labels). From Heavy Metal (studio 3:30). Piano replaces the studio French horn (Paste).
7. Cancer of the Skull 07:03 - From Heavy Metal (studio 5:31). Over 7 minutes. The only song Winter introduces to the crowd (Paste).
8. If You Turn Back Now 05:37 - New, unreleased song (not on Heavy Metal). Paste calls it a ballad.
9. Nina + Field of Cops 06:37 - FCC “shit” x1 (Verse 2). From Heavy Metal (studio 5:52). Paste calls it a crowd favorite.
10. $0 08:42 - FCC “fuck” x2 (Intro). From Heavy Metal (studio 6:43). Longest track, over 8 minutes.
11. Take It With You 06:17 - From the 2024 Singles EP, not Heavy Metal (studio 4:01).
12. Vines 04:38 - From the 2024 Singles EP, not Heavy Metal (studio 3:06). Closer.
