Release Notes:

Caspian Halliwell - lead vocals, guitar, songwriter; RJ Santos - steel guitar (Sub Pop and press profiles). The two met as neighbors in New Orleans' Holy Cross neighborhood. Halliwell's earlier bands include Blackbird Raum and Scissorbills; Santos played in Cajun and country bands.
No producer, studio or other personnel credits published as of Oct. 18.
Sound: garage rock, country and traditional folk, with steel guitar over shuffling drums (Pitchfork, via Sub Pop). Earlier songs lean on drum machines, slide guitar, organ and a drawling baritone (Stereogum).
Sub Pop debut, after two earlier 2026 albums, Blame the Clown (Chain Smoking Records) and Florida Water Blues (Going Underground).
Press materials call the songs anthems of longing and disappointment, from a brutal breakup to snitching ex-friends.
