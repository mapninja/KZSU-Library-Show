Release Notes:

Not yet released. Out Nov. 6 on Sub Pop. Third Twisted Teens album of 2026, after Blame the Clown (Chain Smoking Records, January) and Florida Water Blues (Going Underground, July; Sub Pop outside North America in September). First album since the band signed to Sub Pop in August.
New Orleans duo Caspian Halliwell and RJ Santos lead the band (Sub Pop). 13 tracks. No producer, studio or personnel credits had been published as of Oct. 18.
First single When We First Met arrived Sept. 10 with the album announcement; Sub Pop calls it a 113-second tale of a love long gone. Video for the opener Stalks followed Oct. 7.
Pitchfork put the album on its 34 most anticipated albums of fall 2026 list, citing the band's strut through garage rock, country and traditional folk (as quoted by Sub Pop).
LP preorders from Sub Pop and at shows get the Loser Edition on maroon vinyl; there is also a Bandcamp exclusive LP, a CD and a cassette.
On tour with Kurt Vile and The Violators from Nov. 4 (Buffalo) to Nov. 24 (Baltimore), after a Nov. 3 headline show at Bowery Ballroom in New York. Australian dates follow in December.
Lyrics are posted for only two tracks (1 and 2), both clean. Tracks 3 through 13 are unscreened.
