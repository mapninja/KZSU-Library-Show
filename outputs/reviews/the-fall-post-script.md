Release Notes:

Billed as the band's final studio album. Nine tracks built from unused Mark E. Smith vocal and lyric recordings made between 2001 and 2014, set to new backing tracks. Three vocals are by Ed Blaney and a couple by Simon "Ding" Archer (Louder Than War).
Assembled by Smith confidants Ed Blaney (friend, solo artist) and Simon "Ding" Archer (former Fall bassist, long-time Fall engineer at 6DB studio; credits include PJ Harvey and Pixies). Cog Sinister / Gonzo, cat. COGGZ154 (LP, CD).
Backing tracks run to industrial and metallic noise rather than the band's late-period garage sound (Louder Than War). Reviewers split on the Fall name: Progressive Aspect calls it "Fall by numbers" and finds Smith's vocals largely impenetrable, and Real Gone notes repeated cut-up vocals.
Disputed release: Smith's fiancee and last manager Pamela Vander called it ethically wrong and says Smith named New Facts Emerge (2017) as the final album. Dork reports the album has the approval of Smith's estate. Reports conflict, so flag this if you mention it on air.
Release date conflict: Wikipedia and Album of the Year list Oct. 2, 2026. Louder Than War lists Sept. 8. Nine tracks, about 44 minutes.
