Release Notes:

Fourth album from the New York band, and the follow-up to Our Brand Could Be Yr Life (Apr. 12, 2024, Chrysalis). The 2025 mini-album Rot in Helvetica came out under the alter-ego name NODEGA.
Ben Hozie - guitar, vocals; Nikki Belfiglio - vocals, percussion, sampling; Dan Ryan - lead guitar; Adam See - bass; Adam Shumski - drums (Wikipedia).
Produced by Matt Peel (Yard Act, Eagulls) at the Nave, his studio in a deconsecrated church in Leeds, England. Recorded live over about six weeks, the band's first time recording outside New York. Mixed in Brighton by Theo Verney (English Teacher, Lime Garden).
Hozie says he never thought of Bodega as post-punk and cites late-'80s and early-'90s Stone Roses and Jane's Addiction as the model, with more riffing and guitar solos. Belfiglio built the miniature fish-tank scene on the cover over five months.
Singles: Pick up the Check (May 6, video directed by Hozie), All Inside Aquarium (June 22, video directed by Belfiglio), Weather Me (Aug. 5), Literary World (Sept. 9) and Slow Train (shared ahead of release, coverage dated Oct. 8).
12 tracks, 45:20 (Bandcamp, AllMusic). Out Oct. 9, 2026. Lyrics are posted on Genius for all 12 tracks, with no section labels.
