Release Date: October 9, 2026

General Comments / Reviews:

Bodega ditches the post-punk tag for late-'80s and early-'90s alt rock, Stone Roses meets Jane's Addiction by the band's own account. Heads up that the title track is FCC. - DJ Stace  [DRAFT, edit or replace]

“much more focused on melodicism than ever before” - Far Out

“they’re not just post-punk; they’re post-genre, and better for it” - SPIN

“sound like Jane’s Addiction covering Bob Dylan” - Glide Magazine

Release Notes:

Ben Hozie - guitar, vocals; Nikki Belfiglio - vocals, percussion, sampling; Dan Ryan - lead guitar; Adam See - bass; Adam Shumski - drums (Wikipedia).
Produced by Matt Peel at the Nave, his studio in a deconsecrated church in Leeds, England. Peel has also worked with Yard Act and produced Eagulls' second album Ullages (Matt Peel site, Wikipedia). Recorded live in the room over about six weeks, the band's first time recording outside New York.
Mixed in Brighton by Theo Verney, who also made the "R&B (Theo Verney Version)" of English Teacher's single (English Teacher Bandcamp).
Lineage: Brooklyn band whose earlier records include Endless Scroll (2018) and Our Brand Could Be Yr Life. Last year the same lineup made the hardcore-leaning Rot in Helvetica as NODEGA (Live4ever).
Hozie says the model is late-'80s and early-'90s Stone Roses meets Jane's Addiction, with more riffing, guitar solos and melody. Songs are more personal than political this time (Far Out, Song Bar).

Tracklist:
1. All Inside Aquarium 04:14 - FCC “fuck” x2 (Genius posts no section labels). Midtempo (~104 BPM). Hazy, chiming guitars over a syncopated rolling rhythm, with a "na-na-na" backing chorus and a psychedelic guitar solo (Song Bar, Glide, SPIN). Hozie lead vocal. Sounds like Stone Roses meets Jane's Addiction (SPIN).
2. Weather Me 03:22 - Mid to uptempo (~124 BPM). Staccato guitars and bass-heavy riffs, bright alt-rock (Song Bar, Glide). Belfiglio lead vocal, semi-spoken, in the vein of Elastica or Karen O (Glide, Live4ever).
3. Literary World 02:59 - Uptempo (~136 BPM). Opens with drum samples, then nimble, jangly guitars; Chills-like (Glide, Song Bar). About empathy in writing fiction (Song Bar).
4. Astro Night Ride 04:57 - Uptempo (~131 BPM). Tempo shifts through the track: suspenseful slow build, accelerating drums and surging distorted guitars, sudden changes of pace, then a gentler landing (Song Bar). Guitars Glide compares to Sonic Youth. Longest track. About recurring dreams of a late mother (Song Bar).
5. Pick up the Check 04:38 - Midtempo (~103 BPM). Brisk, funky rock groove with swirling reverbed guitars and almost-rapped vocal lines (Song Bar, Glide). Hozie vocal. Stone Roses or baggy-era feel (Glide, Live4ever). About leaving a failing relationship (Song Bar).
6. Oxygen 02:54 - Midtempo (~104 BPM). One of the album's more reflective, slacker-rock moments (Glide, Live4ever). Vocals shared by Belfiglio and Hozie (SPIN).
7. Fear not the Fear 04:47 - Midtempo (~103 BPM). Heavily reverbed, shimmering guitars that grow more psychedelic as it goes (Glide, SPIN). Warm Hozie lead vocal (SPIN).
8. Slow Train 03:51 - Tempo source says ~188 BPM, possibly double-time, so check by ear. Cover of "Slow Train" by Bob Dylan (written by Dylan, 1979, title track of Slow Train Coming): Dylan's words, new music by the band. Tougher glam-rock arrangement with a big guitar solo and reverbed vocal, like Jane's Addiction covering Dylan (Glide, SPIN).
9. Combustible 03:11 - Uptempo (~135 BPM). Muscular guitars and insistent rhythm, heavier and more abrasive (Song Bar, Glide).
10. Pile Driver 02:50 - Fast (~172 BPM). Chunky, driving guitars, angular post-punk with an early-'90s alt-rock feel (SPIN, Glide). Belfiglio lead vocal. Shortest track.
11. Backbeaten 04:14 - Caution “asshole” x1 (Genius posts no section labels; near the end of the lyrics). Mid to uptempo (~123 BPM). Catchy looping melody over frenetic riffs, lush but with steady old-school rock momentum (SPIN).
12. Olivia 03:23 - Midtempo (~109 BPM). Closer. Bright guitars under a lazy, Courtney Barnett-style Belfiglio vocal (Glide). Reflective, intimate singer-songwriter feel (SPIN). Written about a cousin who died (Song Bar).
