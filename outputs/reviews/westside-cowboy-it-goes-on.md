Release Notes:

Debut album from the Manchester four-piece, after two EPs. Produced, engineered and mixed by Loren Humphrey, who has also worked with Geese and Cameron Winter.
Reuben Haycocks - guitar, vocals; Jimmy Bradbury - guitar, vocals; Aoife Anson-O'Connell - bass, vocals; Paddy Murphy - drums, vocals. Three of them trade lead vocals.
Recorded mostly at Greenmount Studios in Leeds, mixed at Magic Mountain in Tuxedo Park, NY, and mastered by Felix Davis at Metropolis in London. The last two tracks come from pre-production sessions in Humphrey's upstate New York living room.
The band calls its sound 'Britainicana': British indie rock crossed with American folk and alt-country. Formed in 2023; three of them met at the Royal Northern College of Music.
The songs keep returning to time passing and things in motion, though Haycocks says it is not a concept album. The band wanted it to sound young, immediate and a little rough.

Tracklist:
1. Kick Stones (The Boys) 03:56 - Uptempo (~139 BPM). Slow-build arrangement modeled on a live Velvet Underground 'What Goes On' (band). VU fuzz sped up and sharpened (Pitchfork).
2. Paper Chains 02:51 - Fast (~162 BPM). Listed as 'Paperchains' on Apple Music and Genius. Vampire Weekend-style guitar trills in the breakdown (Pitchfork); chaotic drumming at the end (The Soundboard).
3. Dobro 02:45 - Mid to uptempo (~128 BPM). Written by Bradbury on a four-track cassette recorder. Murphy calls it the band's first 'strutting' song.
4. Well Done Kid, You Did It 03:27 - Uptempo (~137 BPM). Written under the influence of Modest Mouse; Haycocks calls it an 'emo dance track'.
5. Worried Age 03:58 - Fast (~169 BPM). Began as a 10-minute jam inspired by LCD Soundsystem and New Order (band). Future Islands shaped the final version. Called the album's centerpiece (Pitchfork).
6. Big Wheels 03:17 - Slow to midtempo (~77 BPM). Bradbury song about repetition and feeling stuck. Blockbuster DVD imagery (NME).
7. Pin Up Boys 02:45 - Tempo check: songbpm says ~90 BPM, but Pitchfork calls it a rousing anthem and the band cut it essentially live, so it may be half-time. Abrasive, raw (band, Pitchfork); loose (Rolling Stone UK).
8. Coyote 03:18 - FCC: fucking x1 (final chorus). Tempo check: songbpm says ~182 BPM, but the band calls it sparse and percussion-driven, so it may be double-time. Haycocks and Anson-O'Connell share vocals. Sweet and sad (Pitchfork). Only track Apple Music marks Explicit.
9. Patchwork 03:22 - Tempo check: songbpm says ~142 BPM, but critics place it with the quiet ballads, so it may be half-time. Bradbury song for guitar and voice, with stray piano notes and audible finger noise on the strings (Pitchfork).
10. Take My Leaving As I Love You 02:43 - Slow to midtempo (~88 BPM). Stripped-down early take from pre-production at Humphrey's upstate New York house. Balladry (Rolling Stone UK). Sources list ~84 to ~93 BPM.
11. You Could Have Died There On The Dancefloor 02:43 - Midtempo (~107 BPM). Closer. Early demo with new vocals and an added guitar (band).
