Sprawling, noisy, horn-flecked indie rock with Americana roots, produced by John Congleton. Record Collector hears Fugazi-style post-hardcore in the heavy parts, and three tracks are FCC, the opener included. - DJ Stace  [DRAFT, edit or replace]

Release Notes:

Third album from Burlington, Vermont singer-songwriter Greg Freeman, after I Looked Out (July 2022) and Burnover (Aug. 2025). Transgressive says Burnover made The New York Times' best albums of 2025 and earned Pitchfork's Best New Album tag.
13 tracks, about 67 minutes, spread over four sides of a double LP. Recorded April 8 to 18, 2026 in Los Angeles. Produced, mixed and engineered by John Congleton; mastered by Matt Colton. All songs written by Freeman.
Greg Freeman - guitar, vocals; Garrett Linck - bass, group vocals; Joey Sullivan (Florry) - drums, percussion; Carney Hemler (The Rockin Worms) - guitar, group vocals; Sam Atallah - piano, keys, trumpet, horn arrangements, group vocals; Scott Daniel - violin.
Guests: Anna McClellan and Trinity Ace (vocals), David Ralicke (saxophone), Vikram Devasthali (trombone), Mike Bolger (accordion on track 13).
Written while Freeman left his longtime Burlington home and went full-time in music, after jobs as a cemetery landscaper and restaurant line cook (Transgressive). The label name-checks early Pavement and pre-Good News Modest Mouse, with Fugazi and Unwound behind the heavier songs.
Singles: Indu (July 21, with the album announcement), Cahokia (Aug. 26) and Loser's Prize (Sept. 29, the final preview). North American tour starts Oct. 20, with a San Francisco stop at The Independent on Nov. 5.
Lyrics are posted for all 13 tracks. Three have FCC words: 1, 11 and 13.

Tracklist:
1. Cahokia 04:32 - FCC “fucked” x1 (Verse 1); “shit” x1 (final Chorus). Opener. Second single (Aug. 26). Vocals by Anna McClellan.
2. Totaled 04:43 - Vocals by Anna McClellan. Paste says it grieves a towed Toyota. Record Collector hears 90s alt-rock noise.
3. Culture 05:24 - Vocals by Trinity Ace. Paste calls it syncopated, a military march toward doom.
4. Withdrawn 05:36 - Vocals by Anna McClellan and Trinity Ace. Saxophone: David Ralicke; trombone: Vikram Devasthali. Paste says it starts sparse and acoustic and ends as a horn-filled anthem.
5. Indu 04:21 - Lead single (July 21). Vocals by Anna McClellan. Paste calls it a pop earworm; Record Collector hears psych-y slacker garage.
6. Loser's Prize 04:42 - Final single (Sept. 29). Vocals by Anna McClellan. Paste calls it uptempo yet melancholy.
7. Hail Mary 05:35 - Vocals by Anna McClellan. Per Paste, the first two minutes are a slow instrumental before the riffs arrive. Record Collector calls it a highlight, Fugazi-style post-hardcore.
8. Decade 03:55 - Shortest track. Saxophone: David Ralicke; trombone: Vikram Devasthali. Paste hears Dexys Midnight Runners in the horn bridge.
9. Marooned 06:54 - Longest track. Vocals by Trinity Ace. Paste says it opens as a minor-key dirge and builds to a full arrangement. Record Collector calls it pounding, Fugazi-style post-hardcore.
10. Untitled 10 05:10 - Saxophone: David Ralicke; trombone: Vikram Devasthali. Per Paste, opens on fuzzed-up guitar and turns into a ballad in the second half. Record Collector calls it a brass-imbued psych wig-out.
11. Next To Nothing 04:18 - FCC “shit” x1 (early in song; Genius posts no sections).
12. Costs 05:46 - Vocals by Anna McClellan and Trinity Ace.
13. Bury Me The Shovel 05:45 - FCC “shit” x1 (early in song; Genius posts no sections). Closer. Accordion: Mike Bolger. Vocals by Anna McClellan and Trinity Ace. Record Collector calls it a country-ish ballad.
