Label: Transgressive Records / Canvasback

Release Date: October 2, 2026
Review Date: 10/18/2026

General Comments / Reviews:

Sprawling, noisy, horn-flecked indie rock with Americana roots, swinging from slacker garage to Fugazi-style post-hardcore, produced by John Congleton. Three tracks are FCC, the opener included. - DJ Stace  [DRAFT, edit or replace]

“widescreen Americana-founded experiential rock” - Record Collector

“a perfect pop earworm” - Paste

Release Notes:

Greg Freeman is a Burlington, Vermont singer-songwriter; third album, after I Looked Out (2022) and Burnover (2025). Written as he left Burlington for the Southwest and went full-time in music; he calls it a reflection of a lot of change and tension (Bandcamp). Label cites early Pavement and pre-Good News Modest Mouse, with Fugazi and Unwound behind the heavy songs.
Greg Freeman - guitar, vocals; Garrett Linck - bass, group vocals; Joey Sullivan (Florry) - drums, percussion; Carney Hemler (The Rockin Worms) - guitar, group vocals; Sam Atallah - piano, keys, trumpet, horn arrangements, group vocals; Scott Daniel - violin.
Guests: Anna McClellan (vocals, tracks 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 7, 12, 13); Trinity Ace (vocals, tracks 3, 4, 9, 12, 13); David Ralicke (saxophone) and Vikram Devasthali (trombone) on tracks 4, 8, 10; Mike Bolger (accordion, track 13).
Produced, mixed and engineered by John Congleton in Los Angeles (assistant engineer Rachel White); mastered by Matt Colton. All songs written by Freeman.
Congleton fronted the Dallas alt-rock band The Paper Chase and has produced St. Vincent (Actor, Strange Mercy, the self-titled album), and worked with Angel Olsen, Explosions in the Sky, Modest Mouse and Okkervil River.

Tracklist:
1. Cahokia 04:32 - FCC “fucked” x1 (Verse 1); “shit” x1 (final Chorus). Midtempo (~96 BPM). Opens like a road song on chiming guitars, then builds slowly to urgent, stormy guitars and drums (Song Bar, Americana Highways, Fire Note). Anna McClellan on vocals. About the ancient Mississippian site and modern decline (Americana Highways).
2. Totaled 04:43 - Mid to uptempo (~125 BPM). Ragged 90s-style guitar noise with a pace-changing rhythm (Song Bar). Recalls early Nirvana (Americana Highways), the Replacements or early Modest Mouse (Fire Note). Anna McClellan on vocals. About a wrecked, towed Toyota (Paste).
3. Culture 05:24 - Midtempo (~102 BPM). Driving, abrasive guitars and a syncopated, chugging rhythm that crashes into brass and a wall of noise (Song Bar, Paste, Americana Highways). Clatters like Califone (PopMatters). Trinity Ace on vocals.
4. Withdrawn 05:36 - Slow to midtempo (~84 BPM). Starts quiet with strummed acoustic guitar and sparse percussion, then adds horns, violin and piano and ends as a horn anthem (Paste, Song Bar). Saxophone: David Ralicke; trombone: Vikram Devasthali. Anna McClellan and Trinity Ace on vocals.
5. Indu 04:21 - Midtempo (~105 BPM). Spacious, psychedelic pop with a pumping feel; lead vocal has something of Neil Young (Song Bar). Hooky, pop-leaning (Paste); slacker garage (Record Collector). Anna McClellan on vocals.
6. Loser's Prize 04:42 - Fast (~167 BPM). Stomping, twangy guitar and organ, with energetic guitar and piano solos (Northern Transmissions, Americana Highways). Upbeat but melancholy (Paste); Old 97's swagger (Fire Note). Anna McClellan on vocals.
7. Hail Mary 05:35 - Midtempo (~95 BPM). About two minutes of slow, tentative guitar and violin before the band erupts into jagged math-rock guitar and post-hardcore distortion; frantic, squalling ending (Paste, Song Bar, Americana Highways). Tempo shifts. Fugazi-style (Record Collector). Anna McClellan on vocals.
8. Decade 03:55 - Midtempo (~113 BPM). Horn-accented, groovy rollick with ragtime-style piano; horn bridge recalls Dexys Midnight Runners (Paste, PopMatters, Northern Transmissions). Saxophone: David Ralicke; trombone: Vikram Devasthali. Shortest track.
9. Marooned 06:54 - Tempo sources unclear (one lists ~138 BPM, critics call it plodding). Opens as a quiet minor-key dirge and explodes around the four-minute mark into pounding drums and frantic guitars (Paste, Song Bar, PopMatters). Strings through it (Far Out); Fugazi-style (Record Collector). Longest track. Trinity Ace on vocals.
10. Untitled 10 05:10 - Midtempo (~114 BPM). Opens on heavy, fuzzed-up, minor-key guitar and turns into a ballad in the second half, with organ, mournful violin solo by Scott Daniel and flashes of brass (Paste, Americana Highways, Northern Transmissions). Saxophone: David Ralicke; trombone: Vikram Devasthali.
11. Next To Nothing 04:18 - FCC “shit” x1 (early in song; Genius posts no sections). Midtempo (~95 BPM). Bluesy and catchy (Song Bar). Dense arrangement with the lead vocal sitting low against it (Northern Transmissions).
12. Costs 05:46 - Slow to midtempo (~80 BPM). Bright pub-style piano over a steady groove; piano carries the outro (Northern Transmissions). Anna McClellan and Trinity Ace on vocals.
13. Bury Me The Shovel 05:45 - FCC “shit” x1 (early in song; Genius posts no sections). Midtempo (~105 BPM). Country-ish ballad (Record Collector). Twangy acoustic guitars, accordion by Mike Bolger and violin, with Anna McClellan and Trinity Ace harmonizing (Paste, Northern Transmissions). Closer.
