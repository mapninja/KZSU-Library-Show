Release Notes:

Sixth album from the Los Angeles band. Written after bandleader Bonnie Bloomgarden lost her Altadena home in the Eaton Fire, and her dog Tommy soon after.
Produced by Mark Rains and Bonnie Bloomgarden. Rains engineered and mixed at Station House; Stephen C Common mastered at Twin Hills Recording.
Bonnie Bloomgarden - organ, Juno 60, vocals; Laena Myers - guitar, vocals; Alana Amram - bass; Bailey Chapman - drums; Sarah Safaie - saxophone; Gregg Foreman - Wurlitzer, Rhodes, synth. Foreman died in April 2026 after the record was finished (Louder Than War, Glide).
Guest voices include Laura Kelsey (The Kid), Kelsey Hart (Little Ghost), Hannah Pierce, Seth Bogart (Hunx & His Punx), jazz singer Gretchen Parlato, Shannon Lay and a children's chorus (Bandcamp, AllMusic, Silent Radio).
9 tracks, about 27 minutes. Singles: Message From the Venus Flower (Aug. 11, with the album announcement), Basic Witch (Sept. 2) and How Losing Everything in the Fire Taught Me to Love Myself (Oct. 5). Fire and Brimstone is a Link Wray cover that first came out in 2025 as the B-side of Sisters of the Moon.

Tracklist:
1. Welcome to Earth 03:08 - Opener. Layers of wah-wah guitar, synths and sax (Glide, AllMusic).
2. Basic Witch 03:09 - Features Seth Bogart. Second single (Sept. 2), with a video directed by Henry Mortensen and starring Exene Cervenka. Already on the Weekly playlist. Garage-rocking groove (Glide); Wurlitzer and sax (Silent Radio).
3. Plant Magic 02:50 - Dedicated to renewal (AllMusic). Guest choir of children and singers including Shannon Lay and Gretchen Parlato (Silent Radio). Sax-laced punk energy (Glide, Louder Than War).
4. Sing For Yourself 03:45 - Longest track. Swaying tempo with spaghetti-western guitar (Spill); jazzy rhythms (Silent Radio). Glide finds it wandering and one-note.
5. Message From the Venus Flower 02:42 - Lead single (Aug. 11), with a video directed by Ian Svenonius. Tribute to Bloomgarden's dog Tommy (AllMusic). Funkier (AllMusic); new wave feel (Glide).
6. I love you 02:50 - Tense and revving (Glide); Riot Grrrl-style exuberance (Louder Than War).
7. Fire and Brimstone 03:54 - Cover of Link Wray (written by Wray, from his 1971 album). First released in 2025 as the B-side of Sisters of the Moon, a Fleetwood Mac cover. Sarah Safaie on saxophone. Spill calls it the best song; Glide hears a 70s disco groove.
8. Life So Far 02:05 - Shortest track. Girl-group vibes (Glide); surf-guitar riff (Spill).
9. How Losing Everything in the Fire Taught Me to Love Myself 02:55 - Closer and final single (Oct. 5), with a Dylan Mars Greenberg video. Ends the album on a message of hope (Louder Than War).
