Release Notes:

Sixth album from the Los Angeles garage-psych band (formed 2013; earlier records include Darkness Rains, Under the Spell of Joy and Islands in the Sky). Written after Bonnie Bloomgarden lost her Altadena home in the Eaton Fire, and her dog Tommy soon after. About loss, connection and renewal.
Produced by Mark Rains and Bonnie Bloomgarden. Rains engineered and mixed at Station House (Echo Park); Stephen C Common mastered at Twin Hills Recording. Rains also produced the band's debut Street Venom and Darkness Rains, their first record for Suicide Squeeze.
Bonnie Bloomgarden - organ, Juno 60, vocals; Laena Myers - guitar, vocals; Alana Amram - bass; Bailey Chapman - drums; Sarah Safaie - saxophone; Gregg Foreman - Wurlitzer, Rhodes, synth. Foreman died in April 2026 after the record was finished (Louder Than War, Glide).
Guest voices include Laura Kelsey, Kelsey Hart, Hannah Pierce, Seth Bogart (Hunx & His Punx), jazz singer Gretchen Parlato, Shannon Lay and a children's chorus (Bandcamp, AllMusic, Silent Radio).

Tracklist:
1. Welcome to Earth 03:08 - Mid to uptempo (~120 BPM). Trippy opener: swirling wah-wah guitar, synths, drums and sax, with the female lead vocal buried in haze (Glide, AllMusic). Sounds like Kathleen Hanna auditioning for the Shangri-Las (Silent Radio).
2. Basic Witch 03:09 - Fast (~160 BPM). Features Seth Bogart. Electric organ and a kitschy spoken-word intro, like a 1960s novelty Halloween record (Spill). Wurlitzer, drums and boisterous sax, danceable with big vocal hooks (Silent Radio); garage-rocking groove (Glide).
3. Plant Magic 02:50 - Fast (~168 BPM). Upbeat rumbling drums, sax-laced punk energy (Glide, Louder Than War). Abrasive guitar against sax (Silent Radio); Riot Grrrl exuberance (Louder Than War). Choir of children and singers including Shannon Lay and Gretchen Parlato (Silent Radio). About renewal (AllMusic).
4. Sing For Yourself 03:45 - Slow to midtempo (~84 BPM). Ride cymbal and spaghetti-western guitar give an uneasy, swaying tempo (Spill). Jazzy rhythms and communal group vocals, briefly building to a rock and roll blowout (Silent Radio). Glide finds it wandering.
5. Message From the Venus Flower 02:42 - Uptempo (~130 BPM). Pulsing keys and beats with a hypnotic feel (Silent Radio); funkier (AllMusic); new wave feel with sax (Glide). A tribute to Bloomgarden's dog Tommy (AllMusic).
6. I love you 02:50 - Uptempo (~153 BPM). Tense and revving, upbeat rumbling drums (Glide). Buoyant, loose rock and roll (Silent Radio) with Riot Grrrl exuberance (Louder Than War).
7. Fire and Brimstone 03:54 - Midtempo (~105 BPM). Cover of Fire and Brimstone by Link Wray (written by Wray, 1971). Sarah Safaie on sax. Spacey piano, percussive chimes and a 70s disco groove (Glide); plastic soul like a Bowie Young Americans outtake (Spill). Chanting group vocals up front, fading toward the end (Joyzine).
8. Life So Far 02:05 - Mid to uptempo (~127 BPM). Girl-group vibes with distorted vocals (Glide). Bouncy, crashing drums and a surf-guitar riff (Spill); choir against a jaunty beat (Silent Radio). Short, and it stops cold at the end (Joyzine).
9. How Losing Everything in the Fire Taught Me to Love Myself 02:55 - Mid to uptempo (~125 BPM). Starts ominously with outer-space vibes, then rises (Glide). Sax and many voices (Silent Radio), repeating a refrain about being free (AllMusic). Ends the album on a message of hope (Louder Than War).
