Review Date: 10/8/2026

General Comments / Reviews:

There's something wrong with Robert Pollard. Album number 44, and it rocks like a band half their age. 12 songs, 37 minutes, all killer. - DJ Stace  [DRAFT, edit or replace]

Release Notes:

Lineup: Robert Pollard (vocals), Doug Gillard (lead guitar), Bobby Bare Jr. (rhythm guitar), Mark Shue (bass), Kevin March (drums).
12 songs in about 37 minutes; reviewers note a turn back to loud guitar rock after recent prog-leaning records.
Opener Lost in the Sun. You have aired GBV on 92 shows.
Label not in the Zookeeper label table.
Produced by Travis Harrison (Wikipedia). Pollard says it was recorded in a studio with members living in different places (Rolling Stone).
released May 29, 2026

FCCs: None found

Tracklist:
1. Lost In The Sun 02:17 - Opener: feedback blast into big chopped chords (AllMusic). In the Oct. 8 plan. ecoustics compares it to The Who and The Cars, with Mellotron strings and tight harmonies.
2. Out With A Theory 03:11 - About a cover band that starts writing originals and dreams of recording with Mitch Easter (Pollard). Starts with Pollard and heavy electric guitar; the full band enters about halfway (The Fire Note).
3. One Last Blow 03:09
4. We Outlast Them All 04:23 - Lead single; Pollard calls it GBV's We Are The Champions. Announced with the album Feb. 25, 2026. Pollard says it was written in one take (Rolling Stone). Called a triumphant anthem (Maximum Volume Music).
5. A Grand Ceremonial Jester 01:19 - Sgt. Pepper-style psych pop carnival in 1:18 (The Fire Note).
6. Dagon's Plunger 04:40 - Builds to a strutting riff over sinister vocals (PopMatters). Some of the album's strongest guitar work (Maximum Volume Music).
7. Advance Without Dropping 02:49 - Aggressive punk energy with melodic turns; GBV at their punkiest (Maximum Volume Music).
8. No Shoe Fits (Floating Babies) 02:10
9. Arthur Square 03:44 - New Wave-leaning rocker that veers prog (Maximum Volume Music). ecoustics hears a cousin of the 1969 pre-Tommy Who.
10. Landscaping 03:38 - Called a pretty, psychedelic ballad (PopMatters). Altrevue's favorite.
11. (How Would You Like A) Chariot Ride 03:02 - Chord sequence echoes "The Punk and the Godfather" from The Who's Quadrophenia (ecoustics).
12. When You're My Clown (Nothing Happens) 03:27 - Epic closer: heavy chugging riff, then an acoustic-driven coda (ecoustics).
